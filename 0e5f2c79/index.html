The Advisor Marketing Engine

What Axiom covers each month, the foundation every advisor receives, and the three tiers advisors can buy on top of it.

Prepared for Jerry Corless From Chris Evans August 10, 2026 Internal working document
The short version

The floor

$2,007/mo keeps the operation running: rent, Chris’s AI working environment, and the production tools shared across every advisor account.

The foundation

Every advisor gets a brand, website, and story video, provided by Axiom at no cost to the advisor. Delivery runs about $950 per advisor, plus video (being priced now).

The tiers

Advisors who want more buy it in three tiers: Good, Better, Best. Each is a defined monthly scope the current team can deliver.

To decide

Story video cost, tier pricing, add-on pricing, and the founding cohort. The full list closes this page.

Good is table stakes

The “look like a real firm” baseline. If an advisor doesn’t want Good, they don’t belong on the platform.

Better is the differentiator

Where the recruiting-story assets get created: the podcast, the channel, the client-facing content. Every Better advisor becomes a proof point for the recruit track.

Best is the growth partner

Fewer accounts, deeper spend, real ROI conversations. These are the advisors who can talk lead metrics with us.

The rule

We don’t sell a tier until every piece of it is built and tested. Overselling and underdelivering breaks the model.

8 · 4 · 2Founding cohort — 14 accounts

Proposed first-90-day mix: 8 Good, 4 Better, 2 Best. That’s what the current team supports without breaking delivery. The mix grows each quarter based on what actually delivered.
