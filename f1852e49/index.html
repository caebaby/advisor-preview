The model

How APR works

Everything in the last two parts is one system. A model is three things at once: what you own, how you're paid, and what holds it all up. All three change at Axiom Planning Resources.

i
What you own

Your clients, your brand, your book, yours by contract. Every relationship you build is equity in your practice, not production in someone else's.

ii
How you're paid

Fee-based and recurring. January doesn't erase December. The milestone the model points at is recurring revenue that covers your life before any new sale.

iii
What holds it up

The planning team, the back office, the marketing engine. This is the part most advisors underestimate when they go independent, and the reason ownership lasts here.

The fit runs both directions. This model is for advisors who want a fee-based, planning-led practice of their own, and who believe the work is done client-first. If that's you, and everything you said on our call sounds like it is, the rest of this page is the machinery.
